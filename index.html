Live Simulations

Practice where nothing can break

Pixel-faithful replicas of real workplace software. Mistakes are free here, so they are not expensive on the real system.

Step 1 · Step trackingEvery action followed, in order, like the real workflow.
Step 2 · TimerTime taken, shown honestly; speed is part of competence.
Step 3 · Confidence %Earned by performance; errors and hints lower it.
Step 4 · ConfettiA clean, error-free run gets celebrated.

Why this shelf exists: early in my career I trained customer-care staff on the live CRM, with no practice environment. Every trainee click was real, and one wrong move generated an actual customer complaint. So I build the practice version first.
