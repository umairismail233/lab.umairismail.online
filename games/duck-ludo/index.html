Duck Ludo

Live Analytical Thinking

Race four ducks around the pond, capture rivals on the way, and bring all four home first. Play solo or in a room.

Why here: every roll asks which duck to move, when to chase a capture and when to play safe, so each turn is a decision about risk, timing and position.

How to play

  1. Play solo against bots, or in a room with other players.
  2. Roll the dice, then choose which duck to move.
  3. Land on a rival duck to capture it and send it back to its nest.
  4. A duck already on the track flies to its destination on a 6.
  5. Bring all four of your ducks home first to win.
